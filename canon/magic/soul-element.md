# The Soul-Like Element

**Status:** Proposal awaiting author approval.

## Place in the Elemental Foundation

One of the eight fundamental elements has a soul-like role. Its name and its place among the eight signature combinations remain undecided.

This is an element of the world itself, not a separate substance outside the elemental foundation. Its expressions connect ordered mineral formations, constructed beings, natural life, and eventually circuitry.

## Order Outside Living Beings

Outside a living being, this element expresses itself through order and regular structure. Crystals and the hexagonal arrangement of basalt columns are examples of the kinds of formations associated with it.

Such formations are not thereby alive. Order in matter and the internal governance of a living being are different expressions of the same element. A crystal or basalt formation is not necessarily composed solely of this element; elemental combinations and arrangements can produce its visible expressions.

## Internal Governance

Within an animated being, this element provides the mechanism of internal governance: the organization through which the being's parts function together and its activity is coordinated.

Its role is not inherently obedience to an outside master. A maker's ability to command a golem is distinct from the internal governance that allows the golem to function as a being.

The element can form crystal-like pathways through structures composed of other elements. These pathways provide a working image for how internal governance extends throughout a body: something like fiber optics in their arrangement and something like neurons in their coordinating role. These are analogies, not a requirement that the pathways use ordinary light or reproduce real-world nervous systems.

## Golems and Natural Life

Golem-making uses this element to establish the internal organization that animates and governs a constructed being.

Every living thing also contains this element as an essential part of what makes it alive. In this sense, natural organisms and golems share a fundamental principle: both are bodies whose activity is coordinated through the soul-like element. Calling all living things a kind of golem expresses this underlying kinship, rather than requiring that inhabitants of the world use that terminology.

Natural creatures were made by the gods. Their complexity and individual character far exceed what mortal golem-makers can achieve, to the point that natural life can seem almost to break the laws of physics. That appearance does not, by itself, establish that natural creatures literally violate the world's laws.

The distinction between natural life and mortal construction is therefore not simply possession versus absence of this element. The organization and sophistication of its expression are crucial.

## Later Circuitry

In later generations, this element will also have an essential role in circuitry. Its crystal-like pathways connect that technological development to the same elemental principle already expressed in mineral order and the internal governance of beings.

The exact operation of such circuitry, and the historical steps by which people develop it, remain to be established.

## Open Questions

This proposal does not yet determine:

- The element's name or signature combination.
- The precise rules governing its pathways, signals, and interactions with other elements.
- Whether every living being has visibly identifiable pathways, or how those pathways differ among forms of life.
- How golem-makers add instructions or external command mechanisms to an internally governed being.
- What distinguishes animation, consciousness, personhood, and free will.
- Whether this element persists after death, carries personal identity, or participates in an afterlife. Calling it soul-like does not settle those questions.
- Whether mortals can ever reproduce the full complexity and character of divinely made natural life.
